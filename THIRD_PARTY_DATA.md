# Third-party data and software

## Schneider EVRP-TW instances

The benchmark instances associated with Schneider, Stenger, and Goeke (2014)
are external research data and are not redistributed here. Obtain them from
their authorized source and place verified instance files under
`data/schneider/`.

Reference article:

Michael Schneider, Andreas Stenger, and Dominik Goeke, "The Electric
Vehicle-Routing Problem with Time Windows and Recharging Stations,"
*Transportation Science* 48(4), 2014.
https://doi.org/10.1287/trsc.2013.0490

## Commercial solvers

IBM CPLEX, DOcplex, and Gurobi are optional dependencies. Their installation
and use are governed by their vendors' licenses and are not covered by this
repository's Apache-2.0 license.

## Published papers and reference repositories

PDFs and local reference checkouts are ignored. Citations and repository
revisions may be recorded for provenance, but the third-party bytes are not
part of this distribution. The local Version of Record collection is stored
under ignored `document/literature/`; it is for private research use and must
not be committed, released, or redistributed through this project.

The public bibliographic records are maintained in
[`docs/research/references.bib`](docs/research/references.bib), with a reader-facing index in
[`docs/research/literature.md`](docs/research/literature.md). Bibliographic metadata and DOI
links are attribution records, not permission to redistribute publisher PDFs.
